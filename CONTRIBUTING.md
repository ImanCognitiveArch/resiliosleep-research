# Contributing

ResilioSleep currently accepts research collaboration proposals and documentation feedback rather than unsolicited production-code contributions.

Useful proposals are specific and bounded—for example:

- a validation design against actigraphy or polysomnography;
- a device-compatibility or placement protocol;
- a reproducible missingness/coverage analysis;
- a privacy-preserving interoperability adapter;
- an accessibility, consent, or participant-safety improvement.

Do not submit participant-level data, identifiable screenshots, raw questionnaire exports, APK signing material, or copied proprietary code. Opening an issue does not transfer ownership of your contribution and does not grant access to private project materials.
