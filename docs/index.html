Built for inspectable feasibility

ResilioSleep treats acquisition reliability and clinical validity as different questions. The system records technical evidence needed to audit an overnight session, while the research program deliberately avoids translating phone-derived states into REM, NREM, deep sleep, diagnosis, or treatment claims.

Research boundary
This prototype is not a medical device, diagnostic system, clinical sleep-stage detector, or replacement for polysomnography, actigraphy, professional care, or an independent alarm.

Collaboration

Priority collaborations include physiological validation, Android device calibration, standardized placement protocols, privacy-preserving derived-feature analysis, and reproducible feasibility-study methods.
